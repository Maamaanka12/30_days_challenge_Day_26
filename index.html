Basbaas butter pasta in 20 mins.

Silky garlic-tomato cream, a spoon of green basbaas heat, and charred cherry tomatoes. Weeknight-easy, restaurant-glossy.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. 1

    Boil salty like the sea

    Bring a large pot to a rolling boil. Salt generously, cook pasta 1 min shy of packet. Save 1 cup pasta water.

  2. 2

    Char the tomatoes

    Hot pan + olive oil, tomatoes cut-side down 3 mins until blistered. Scoop out, keep the fond.

  3. 3

    Build the base

    Lower heat. Butter, shallot 2 mins, garlic 30 secs, tomato paste 2 mins until brick-red and glossy.

  4. 4

    Cream + basbaas

    Stir in cream and 1 tsp basbaas. Simmer 2 mins. Splash pasta water to loosen to a coating sauce.

  5. 5

    Marry pasta & sauce

    Toss pasta + half the parmesan 2 mins, adding water until it shines. Fold back tomatoes.

  6. 6

    Finish bright

    Off heat: lemon zest + squeeze, torn basil, more basbaas if brave. Top with parmesan, serve now.

Cook's note: sauce too thick? Pasta water. Too mild? More basbaas. Too rich? More lemon. Taste three times.
